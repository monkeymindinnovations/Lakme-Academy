About The Academy

Two Industry Stalwarts, One Career Path for You

Lakmé Academy is a partnership between two industry leaders — Lakmé and Aptech — bringing decades of combined experience in beauty and professional training to Ludhiana.

Our job-oriented programs cover every corner of the beauty industry: Makeup, Hair, Cosmetology, Skin Care and Nail Art. Training is built equally around technical skill and the soft skills salons actually hire for, so you leave ready to work — not just certified.

  • Curriculum designed and taught by Lakmé-trained experts
  • Latest equipment and in-demand techniques
  • Pathways into salons, bridal studios, fashion & media
  • Two training centres across Ludhiana
